features:
  - Extract highlights, underlines, notes, comments, and other PDF annotations
  - Filter annotations by type, color, and author
  - Extract exact annotation text or surrounding sentences
  - Export annotations to Markdown, TXT, CSV, Excel, and JSON
  - Export annotations to Obsidian and Notion
  - Process multiple PDF files in a workspace
  - Support XFDF and FDF annotation workflows
  - Process PDF files locally in the browser
