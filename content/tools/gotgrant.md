title: GotGrant
summary: GotGrant is an independent web workspace for researchers exploring NIH funding trends, funded investigators and recently awarded projects. It uses public NIH RePORTER records and links results to the official source. Users can save searches and receive daily or weekly alerts. GotGrant Pro costs US$14.99/month or US$119/year. A narrower postdoc lab-leads plan costs US$9.99/month or US$59/year and highlights recently funded labs, not confirmed vacancies. Subscriptions renew until cancelled. GotGrant is not affiliated with or endorsed by NIH.
