summary: LiterView shows the new articles in your social-science subfields, from the field's journals, in one place. It is for graduate students, postdocs and faculty who need to keep up with new work in their subfields. It covers journal articles published since 2020 in political science, economics, sociology, psychology, public policy and business; working papers and preprints are not included. You can also save articles to a library and read their PDFs, with your highlights and notes kept.
features:
- Browse new articles by discipline, subfield and journal
- Follow subfields and journals in a personal feed
- Save articles to a library and read their PDFs, with highlights and notes
- Get an article's PDF through your library's access or an open copy with the Chrome extension
