title: PerfectRefs
summary: 'PerfectRefs checks each reference in a list against Crossref, OpenAlex and other scholarly databases, flags the ones it can''t find, and shows where the author, title, year, journal, volume, issue, pages or DOI differ from the matched record. It is for students and researchers checking a thesis or paper before submission. The existence check is free for 10 references a day with a free account; the field-by-field comparison uses paid credits.


  Disclosure: I built PerfectRefs.'
features:
- 'Existence check: each reference marked Found, Needs checking or Not found, with a link to the matched record'
- Looks up by DOI (resolved at doi.org) or by title, authors and year
- Field-by-field comparison with the database record; accept a correction in one click
- Exports the corrected list in APA 7, MLA 9, Chicago author-date, IEEE, Vancouver or GB/T 7714
- Imports .docx, .bib, .txt, .ris and .enw files
