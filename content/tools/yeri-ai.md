summary: 'Yeri AI is a browser-based image and video creation tool. Researchers and science communicators can use its image generation and editing workflows to draft illustrative visuals for talks, teaching and outreach, then adapt them into short videos. It is a general creative tool, not a scientific measurement or validation system: generated visuals should be checked and labelled appropriately, and should not be presented as experimental evidence. A free plan is available, with paid options for additional usage.'
