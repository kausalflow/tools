title: Glasp
summary: Glasp is a browser extension and web app for highlighting and annotating what you read online. Researchers and students use it to highlight web articles and PDFs, add notes, and keep everything in one searchable place. It also shows the transcript of a YouTube video with an AI summary, which helps with lectures and talks. Highlights and notes export to Markdown, Notion, Obsidian and Roam. It has a free plan.
features:
- Highlight and annotate web pages and PDFs
- YouTube transcripts with AI summaries
- Kindle highlight import
- Export to Markdown, Notion, Obsidian and Roam
- Free plan, and a free plan for verified university students
- iOS and Android apps
