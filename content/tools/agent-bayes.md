summary: Agent Bayes is an AI research assistant for MA and PhD students, postdocs and other academic researchers. You add your own papers as PDFs or sync them from Zotero, then ask a question. The agent searches only your library and writes the answer as a mindmap of claims, each cited to the passage and page it came from. Click a citation to read the passage or open the PDF at that page. When papers disagree, both positions stay on the map with their own sources.
