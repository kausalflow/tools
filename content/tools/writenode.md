title: ⚪️ writenode
summary: A Chrome extension for researchers who read more than they can remember. It captures webpages, highlights and voice notes into a personal knowledge base, and its AI assistant answers questions using your own captured knowledge rather than generic internet results. A live knowledge graph shows how your saved ideas connect.
features:
- Floating note capture on any webpage (text, highlights, full pages)
- Voice capture with transcription
- AI assistant grounded in your own notes
- Knowledge graph of saved ideas
- Rich-text editor with AI actions
- Bring your own AI keys (stay on device)
