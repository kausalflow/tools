title: Research Logbook
summary: Research Logbook is a free Windows beta for students and researchers to keep dated project notes, captioned pictures, source references and next actions together. Notes are stored as readable Markdown and local pictures in a folder the user chooses. It has no separate app account or built-in cloud sync. Three starter notebooks include blank layouts and clearly fictional examples. Submitted by Dimas Visser, the developer.
