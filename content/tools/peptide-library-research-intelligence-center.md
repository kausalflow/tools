title: Peptide Library Research Intelligence Center
summary: 'Open bibliometric dataset and dashboard on the published science behind ~179 peptides: about 65,000 PubMed publications (2000 onward) and about 5,000 ClinicalTrials.gov registrations, each classified by evidence class (human clinical, animal, in vitro, computational, review). Counts by peptide, year, research category and study type, plus a trial-level table with status, phase and whether results were posted. Frozen, citable dataset versions (2026.1) in CSV, JSON and XLSX under CC BY 4.0; every search query and classification rule is published on the methodology page. Metadata only, no medical or dosing advice. Disclosure: I''m the maintainer; it is published by Superfime LLC.'
