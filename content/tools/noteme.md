summary: NoteMe is a Chrome extension that empowers you to place notes anywhere on any website and access them all in one convenient popup. features:
- 'Add notes anywhere: Place notes at any location on any webpage.'
- 'Centralized access: View and manage all your notes from a single popup.'
- 'Instant navigation: Click a note to jump directly to its original spot on the website.'
- 'Tab awareness: Automatically opens the correct tab or switches to it if already open.'
- 'Easy sharing: Share notes with others for quick import and collaboration.'
- 'Privacy-first: Your notes remain completely private and never leave your browser.'
- Works fully offline; no APIs or external servers used.
- 'User-friendly interface: Clean, intuitive design for effortless note management.'
