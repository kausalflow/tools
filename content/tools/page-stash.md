title: Page Stash
summary: PageStash is a fast, powerful web capture and research tool designed for analysts, researchers, and anyone who needs to save and organize online information. With one click, it captures full web pages (screenshots, HTML, and text), extracts key content, and organizes everything into a searchable knowledge base. Perfect for investigations, OSINT workflows, and research-heavy tasks.

features:
- Capture full web pages instantly (screenshots + HTML + text extract)
- Automatically save URLs, metadata, timestamps, and page context
- Organize findings with folders, tags, and notes
- Build visual page-relationship graphs for investigations or research
- Search across all saved pages, titles, text, and tags
- '{''Browser extension for one-click capture'': ''Chrome & Firefox''}'
- Ideal for OSINT, threat intelligence, research, and digital evidence preservation
