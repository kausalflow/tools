title: Journal Metrics
summary: Journal Metrics is a web toolkit from LabCat AI for researchers choosing journals and preparing research outputs. It combines searchable journal profiles and comparisons with historical submission-to-acceptance statistics where available, indicative publication charges, and citation metrics computed from OpenAlex. Free tools include a PRISMA flow diagram generator and a survey sample-size calculator. Journal search and these tools are free; optional AI recommendation reports and subscription features are paid.
features:
- Search and compare journal profiles using OpenAlex-derived citation metrics and subject information.
- View historical submission-to-acceptance medians and interquartile ranges from PubMed records where both dates are available, with sample size and coverage shown; these are not first-decision times or guarantees.
- Check indicative article processing charges, with reminders to verify current fees and waivers with the publisher.
- Build PRISMA study-selection flow diagrams with arithmetic checks and local project saving.
- Estimate survey sample sizes for a single proportion, with finite-population correction and response-rate planning.
